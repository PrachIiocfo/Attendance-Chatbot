iOCFO CONSULTING PVT. LTD. — LEAVE & WFH POLICY (Version 2.0, dated 14-11-2025)

OFFICE WORKING HOURS
Official working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, totalling nine hours per day, or as per the client's working hours/time zone.
This includes a one-hour break: a 30-minute lunch break and two short breaks of 15 minutes each.
Employees must adhere strictly to these timings and maintain punctuality.
Any exception to timings/working days must be pre-approved by the respective manager and HR via email.
Late arrivals exceeding three instances in a calendar month will be considered as a leave for one day.
Working late hours on a given day does not justify late arrival the next day unless formally approved by the reporting manager.

WEEKLY OFFS
Saturday and Sunday are weekly offs.
Based on business needs or project deadlines, employees may be required to work on weekends.
For Interns and Trainees, Saturdays may be considered working days as communicated by their mentor or reporting manager.

LEAVE POLICY — ELIGIBILITY
All full-time employees are eligible for Casual Leave, Privilege/Earned Leave, and Sick Leave.
Trainees/interns are NOT eligible for Privilege/Earned Leave.
Employees on notice period are NOT eligible for any type of leave.

LEAVE CREDIT PROCESS
The leave calendar follows the April-March cycle.
Leave balances are credited quarterly: April, July, October, and January.
Privilege/Earned Leave carry-forward balance from the previous year is updated in April for existing employees.
All balance can be carried forward to the next quarter within a year; a maximum of 15 Privilege/Earned Leave can be carried forward to the next year.
Paid leaves are granted only if sufficient balance is available.

TYPES OF LEAVE
Casual Leave (CL), Privilege/Earned Leave (PL/EL), Sick Leave (SL), Leave Without Pay/Absent (LWP).

LEAVE ENTITLEMENT (per year, across four quarters)
Casual Leave (CL): 1 day per quarter, total 4 days per year. PAID. Carry forward to next year: 0. Minimum applied: 0.5 day. Submit on Zoho People. Approved by Reporting Manager. For unplanned situations/events.
Privilege/Earned Leave (PL/EL): 3 days per quarter, total 12 days per year. PAID. Carry forward to next year: up to 15. Minimum applied: 1 day. Submit on Zoho People. Approved by Reporting Manager. For personal reasons, personal business, or vacation.
Sick Leave (SL): 1 day per quarter, total 4 days per year. PAID. Carry forward to next year: 0. Minimum applied: 0.5 day. Submit on Zoho People. Approved by Reporting Manager. For any health issue or sickness.
Leave Without Pay (LWP)/Absent: UNPAID. Submit on Mail. Approved by Reporting Manager/Project Head. When existing leave balance is exhausted and leave is needed due to unforeseen circumstances.

PAID LEAVE RULES
Paid leaves apply only when adequate balance exists in the respective leave type.
All paid leave requests must be approved by the reporting manager and HR.
Leave requests must be submitted via Zoho People.

UNPAID LEAVE RULES
Leave Without Pay (LWP) can be availed only when no paid leave balance remains, or there is an emergency requiring immediate absence.
Excessive or repeated LWP may be reviewed by HR.

LEAVE APPLICATION PROCESS
All leave requests must be submitted through Zoho People only; no verbal or informal messages accepted.
Apply for leave at least 7 days in advance, except in emergencies.
The request must mention leave type, dates, and a valid reason.
Requests are routed to the reporting manager and HR for approval.
Leave is approved only after the official approval notification from Zoho People.
Any leave taken without prior approval is marked as Leave Without Pay (LWP).
For emergency leave, inform reporting manager and HR the same day and apply on Zoho.
Medical leave beyond two consecutive days may require a doctor's certificate.

COMPENSATORY OFF POLICY
A compensatory leave can be claimed if an employee works on an iOCFO holiday, if: pre-approval to work was taken from the client's manager and reporting manager; no extra leave is claimed if following the client's calendar; the leave is taken within 30 days of the holiday worked.

iOCFO HOLIDAYS
The iOCFO Holiday List is published every year in January on Zoho People.
Employees are entitled to 4 National Holidays and 7 Public Holidays.
The company also provides 1 Optional Holiday, which must be pre-approved.

CANCELLATION OF LEAVE
Approved leave can be cancelled depending on business demand.
When cancelled by the reporting manager, an automatic notification reaches the employee and HR.
Leave balance is updated accordingly on Zoho.

WORK FROM HOME (WFH) POLICY — ELIGIBILITY
Consultants are eligible for up to 10 WFH days per quarter.
Trainees are NOT eligible for WFH.
WFH is not allowed during the notice period, except under management-approved exceptional circumstances.

WFH APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
WFH is not an entitlement; it is granted case-by-case.
Every WFH request must include a valid reason.
Approval must be obtained from the assigned mentor and one of the Directors before availing WFH.
All approvals must follow the company's official communication process.

WFH USAGE RULES
WFH days cannot be carried forward to the next quarter.
Any exception requires strong justification and approval from both the reporting manager and Director.

WFH APPLICATION PROCESS
WFH must be applied through Zoho People, like other leaves.
Approval requests are automatically sent to the reporting manager and Mr. Shashikant Khemani.
Upon approval, the employee receives a notification from Zoho.
Approved WFH days are deducted from the employee's WFH balance, visible on Zoho.

EXTENDED WFH (MEDICAL EMERGENCY)
For extended WFH due to illness, submit a doctor's prescription and relevant medical history documents.
Approval is granted only after verification and management review.

HR COMMUNICATION PROTOCOL
All approvals, leave requests, WFH requests, special requests, or suggestions must include HR and ADMIN in the email.
Any request or approval not marked to HR will not be considered valid.
If an employee fails to inform HR, the reporting manager must ensure HR is informed.

GENERAL GUIDELINES
All employees must fill timesheet in Zoho People before the last working day of every month, else salary will be delayed.
All leave records are maintained in Zoho People.
All leave/WFH requests must be applied on Zoho People before proceeding. In emergencies, written intimation to the reporting manager and HR via mail must be done, and regularized within 2 days of resuming duty.
Employees may apply for leave depending on available balance on Zoho People.
An employee shall not proceed on leave until approved by the reporting manager and HR.
Absence without approved leave for more than 3 working days is considered absconding; HR will take suitable action.
For prolonged illness/absence, the employee must inform HR at regular intervals about their condition and probable return date.
Weekends and holidays falling between sanctioned leave periods are excluded and not counted as leave for Casual, Sick, and Privilege leave.
For planned leave, apply 7 days in advance with manager approval.
Compensatory offs need prior approval from the reporting manager and must be claimed within one month of the day worked.
All leave/comp-off/WFH requests must be communicated via email and Zoho to the reporting manager and HR.
Failure to follow the process or obtain prior approval may lead to disciplinary action.
Exception to this policy applies when employees follow the client's calendar or time zone.
